# Summer-Olympics-Dashboard-1896-2024-Power-BI-Project

From Athens 1896 to Paris 2024

📊 Overview

This Power BI project presents an interactive and visually rich dashboard analyzing Summer Olympic Games from 1896 to 2024.
It showcases trends in athlete performance, medal distributions, participating countries, and evolution in sports across more than a century.

The dashboard is designed to enable users to:

Explore medals by country, year, and sport

Track historical performance of athletes and nations

Compare gender participation trends over time

Visualize global dominance through maps and leaderboards

🔧 Tools & Technologies

Power BI Desktop

DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)

Power Query (ETL)

📁 Dataset Fields

Athlete Name

Gender

Country / NOC

Year, City, Season

Sport & Event

Medal (Gold, Silver, Bronze)

📊 Dashboard Analysis & Key Insights

1. United States – A Century of Dominance
   
The United States consistently leads in total medal count, especially in:

Athletics, Swimming, Rowing

In many Olympic years (e.g., 1984, 2008, 2024), the U.S. topped the chart by a large margin.

The U.S. also leads in gold medals, confirming both quantity and quality.

🔍 Insight:

The U.S. has dominated Summer Olympics for over a century, showcasing global sports leadership, especially in individual athletic events.

👨‍🦱👩 2. Gender Participation Evolved Over Time

From 1896 to 1920, male athletes made up nearly 100% of participants.

After 1972, a steady rise in female participation is visible.

In 2020, female athletes won 49.33% of the total medals, and this share increased to 50.28% in 2024 — marking the first time women surpassed men in Olympic medal wins.

🔍 Insight:

The Olympics evolved from being male-dominated to becoming one of the most gender-inclusive global sports events.

🏃 3. Athletics – The Medal Powerhouse
Athletics stands out as the highest medal-generating sport across all years.

It's followed by Swimming, Gymnastics, and Rowing.

🔍 Insight:

Athletics is the central sport of the Summer Olympics, offering the widest range of medal opportunities.

📈 4. 2020 (Tokyo) – Post-COVID Rebound & Record Participation
Despite delays, Tokyo 2020 had:

High total medal counts

Significant gender balance

The Tokyo 2020 Olympics marked a historic milestone as San Marino, Burkina Faso, and Turkmenistan each won their first-ever Olympic medals.

New sports (e.g., Skateboarding, Sport Climbing) introduced fresh medal contenders.

🔍 Insight:

Tokyo 2020 set new records in gender equality and global medal diversity, proving Olympic resilience post-COVID.

🌍 5. Medal Distribution Becoming More Global

Early Olympics were dominated by Europe and North America.

From 2000 onward, more countries from Asia, Africa, and Latin America appear in medal tables.

Countries like United States,Great Britain,France,Italy,Sweden have become regular medal winners.

🔍 Insight:

The global spread of Olympic success has increased, reflecting more equitable competition and sports development worldwide.

✅ Summary 

This dashboard provides a 128-year historical view of Summer Olympics, revealing clear patterns in national dominance, gender evolution, and sport trends. Key takeaways include U.S. dominance, the rise of female athletes, the medal-rich role of Athletics, and the growing global distribution of Olympic success.
